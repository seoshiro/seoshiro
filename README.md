### 01 / PERCH — Room & furnishing studio

**Try a few layouts before moving a single chair.**

[![PERCH's actual studio: an original furnished 3D room beside its dimensioned 2D plan, furniture catalog, and fit checks](assets/perch.png)](https://seoshiro.github.io/perch-studio/)

Set up a rectangular room, resize furniture to match what you own, and compare independent layouts in linked 2D and 3D views. Export a dimensioned plan, a 3D image, or a printable report.

**[Try PERCH ↗](https://seoshiro.github.io/perch-studio/)** · [Read the source](https://github.com/seoshiro/perch-studio) · [Case study](https://seoshiro.github.io/projects/perch.html)

<sub>React · TypeScript · Three.js · SVG · Local browser saves · EN / RU / KK</sub>

### 02 / SELVEDGE — Apparel workroom

**One graphic, a considered collection.**

[![Actual SELVEDGE workroom with an original After Hours tee illustration, artwork placement controls, and garment colorways](assets/selvedge-workroom.png)](https://seoshiro.github.io/selvedge-studio/)

Place artwork, explore garment colors, and compare named revisions. Export a collection PNG, a PDF visual proof, or an editable project to continue later. The proof helps a conversation with a printer; it is not a manufacturing specification.

**[Try SELVEDGE ↗](https://seoshiro.github.io/selvedge-studio/)** · [Read the source](https://github.com/seoshiro/selvedge-studio) · [Case study](https://seoshiro.github.io/projects/selvedge.html)

### 03 / FORME — Visual research studio

## Tools for careful work

**[GuideCheck](https://seoshiro.github.io/guidecheck/)** — Compare instruction revisions and keep human review evidence attached to the steps it belongs to. Portable workspace backups; EN / RU / KK. [Source](https://github.com/seoshiro/guidecheck)

**[ArchiveGuard](https://seoshiro.github.io/archiveguard/)** — Compare JPEG capture metadata with Google Photos sidecars, resolve conflicts, and export checked copies with an audit trail. Original files stay untouched. [Source](https://github.com/seoshiro/archiveguard)

## How I create

**Define → direct → try → refine.**

I use AI for implementation and problem solving, while I guide the product choices and review the result. My focus is the whole experience: what someone is trying to do, how the interface helps, and what they can take away.

The projects are also my learning ground. I’m developing my understanding of TypeScript, React, browser storage, and testing by working through concrete problems with AI assistance.

**In the repositories:** React, TypeScript, Vite, and authored CSS; Three.js, SVG, Canvas, IndexedDB, and Web Workers where the project needs them. Verification includes unit tests, Playwright, and GitHub Actions, with the actual checks and gaps documented per project.

The [portfolio](https://seoshiro.github.io/) brings the work together in English, Russian, and Kazakh.
